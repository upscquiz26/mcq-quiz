How this works
  • Only finished tests and practice sessions of students count, and only each student's first answer to a question.
  • A student counts once they have answered {{ rules.min_answers }} or more questions; their strength is their accuracy on all the other questions.
  • A question needs {{ rules.min_responders }}+ such students. The high scorers are the top {{ rules.high_share }}% of them (at least {{ rules.min_high }}).
  • It is raised when more than half of the high scorers chose one option that is not the key, beating the key's count by at least {{ rules.margin }} students.

It needs real student data, so it stays quiet on a small class. A raised question is not necessarily wrong — a genuinely tricky question can fool strong students too.
